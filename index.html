why

a dead coin still pays its dev.

every trade on a pump.fun coin pays a creator fee. when the dev walks away, the coin keeps trading and the fees keep landing in a wallet nobody watches. the holders who stayed get nothing.

01

the dev walks

no posts, no updates, no moves from the dev wallet. the chart is all that's left.

02

the fees keep coming

the coin still trades. every buy and every sell still pays the creator fee, to a wallet that never moves.

03

heir hands them over

one missed check-in and the fees go to the 50 wallets that kept holding. no vote, no admin, no second chance.

how it works

four steps. one of them comes back every 72 hours.

heir has no token and no contract of its own to trust. it's notes on Solana and a vault per coin, and every move shows on Solscan.

heir://steps.exe01 / 04
01

add your coin

paste your pump.fun contract address in the app and connect the wallet that created it. heir reads the creator straight from the coin's bonding curve, so only the real dev can add it. you sign one note: heir:1:register.

02

route the fees

heir gives your coin its own vault address. on pump.fun, send your creator fees there. while you're around, the vault pays it all straight back to you.

03

check in

once every 72 hours, sign heir:1:checkin from the dev wallet. it's a note on Solana: one signature plus the network fee. the timer goes back to 72:00:00.

04

miss one

72 hours with no check-in and the switch fires, for good. from then on the vault pays the top 50 holders by bag, every time fees come in. late check-ins don't count.

your cut

what the switch pays you.

after it fires, each payout splits the vault by bag among the top 50 wallets. pools, program accounts, the dev and the vault never count.

on-chain

the chain is the database.

heir keeps no list of its own. every coin, every check-in and every payout is a transaction anyone can read and rebuild.

your wallet

signs the notes

registering and checking in are memo notes from the dev wallet, sent with a 0-SOL transfer to the heir tag address so they're easy to find.

a vault per coin

holds the fees

each coin gets its own vault. its key is derived on heir's server and never leaves it. the code sends a vault's SOL to one of two places only: the dev while alive, the top 50 after.

faq

questions.

the short answers. the docs walk through every rule and every transaction.

does heir need its own token?

no. heir works on any pump.fun coin. the only money that moves is your coin's creator fees, from its vault to the dev or to the heirs.

who holds the vault keys?

each vault's key is derived on heir's server from one secret, and it never leaves the server. the code can send a vault's SOL to exactly two places: the dev while the coin is alive, or its top 50 holders after the switch fires.

what counts as a check-in?

a transaction from the dev wallet carrying the note heir:1:checkin:<mint>. the app builds it for you. one signature, plus Solana's network fee. nothing is paid to heir.

can the dev come back after it fires?

no. the switch fires 72 hours after the last check-in, and it stays fired. check-ins after that are ignored.

how do the fees reach the vault?

pump.fun's fee sharing lets a creator name other wallets for their creator fees. point it at your coin's heir vault, or send fees there yourself. the app shows the vault and the SOL that has landed in it.

when are heirs paid?

anyone can press run payout on the coin's page, and heir sweeps every fired vault once a day. a payout runs when the vault holds at least ◎0.02.
